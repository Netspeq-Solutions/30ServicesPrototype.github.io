Before You Apply

Instructions for Marking Order Application

Welcome to the online Marking Order application service of the Forest and Environment Department, Government of Sikkim. To ensure a smooth application process, please keep the required personal, land, and tree details along with clear copies of your supporting documents ready before you begin.

Required Information Documents You Must Provide Additional Documents, If Applicable
A

Applicant Information

Required Information

You will be asked to provide the following details about the applicant:

  • Applicant’s Full Name: Your first name (and middle or last name, as applicable).
  • Father’s or Husband’s Name: The full name of the applicant’s father or husband.
  • Mobile Number: An active mobile number for receiving application updates and field verification notifications.
  • Who the Application is For: Indicate whether you are submitting the application for yourself or on behalf of another person.
Applying for Yourself (Self) Choose this option if you are applying for a Marking Order in your own name.
Applying on Behalf of Another Person Choose this option if you are assisting a family member or another citizen, and enter that person’s applicant details.
B

Address Information

Required Information

Please keep the applicant’s complete address details ready:

  • Permanent Address: House number, village, ward, or locality details.
  • District: The district in Sikkim where the applicant resides.
  • Block / GPU / Nagar Panchayat / Municipality: Your corresponding Gram Panchayat Unit (GPU), Block, Nagar Panchayat, or Municipality, as applicable.
  • Present Residential Address: You will be asked whether your present residential address is the same as your permanent address. If your current residence is different, please provide your present residential address separately.
C

Forest and Land Information

Information & Supporting Documents

You will need to provide details of the forest jurisdiction, the purpose of tree felling, and the land ownership documents:

1. Forest Jurisdiction & Purpose of Tree Felling Required Information
  • Division and Forest Range: First select your Forest Division, then select the corresponding Forest Range under that division where the land is located.
  • Purpose of Tree Felling: Select the purpose for which tree marking is requested (such as Firewood or Timber).
2. Documents You Must Provide Required Document
  • Land Parcha / Khatiyan: Please provide a clear copy of the Land Parcha or Khatiyan for the plot where the trees are located.
3. Additional Documents, If Applicable Required Only If Applicable
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the Landowner or Revenue Officer: If the Land Parcha / Khatiyan is not in the applicant’s own name, you must provide an NOC from the recorded landowner or the concerned Revenue Officer.
  • NOC / Verification Report from the Panchayat: If there is any land-related dispute or where additional local verification is required, an NOC or Verification Report from the Panchayat may be provided where applicable.
D

Tree Information

Required Information

For each tree you wish to include in the application, please keep the following details ready:

1. Tree Species / Name The local or botanical species name of the tree proposed for marking.
2. Plot / Khasra Number The specific Plot or Khasra number on which the tree stands.
3. GBH (in Feet) The Girth at Breast Height (GBH) of the tree measured in feet.
Important Note: A maximum of 10 trees can be included in one application.
E

Additional Information

Optional

Applicants may also share any additional remarks or relevant details in support of their Marking Order request before submitting the application.
