Overview

About the Marking Order Service

The Marking Order service provides citizens with a unified online entry point to submit requests for tree marking for tree felling in Sikkim through the Forest and Environment Department, Government of Sikkim.

Through this service, applicants can furnish applicant information, district and forest range details, land/plot particulars, tree details, and supporting documents required for departmental verification and review in accordance with applicable Forest Department procedures.

Eligibility

Citizen Helpdesk

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding the Marking Order service in Sikkim

A Marking Order is an official service provided by the Forest and Environment Department, Government of Sikkim, enabling citizens to apply for tree marking for tree felling in accordance with applicable departmental procedures.

Marking Order applications are handled by the Forest and Environment Department, Government of Sikkim.

Citizens seeking a Marking Order for tree felling in Sikkim can apply through this service. Eligibility details are subject to departmental confirmation. Please refer to the official departmental guidelines or contact the department for confirmation.

Applicants must provide the supporting documents prescribed by the Forest and Environment Department. Required document details are pending departmental confirmation. Please refer to the official departmental guidelines or contact the department for confirmation.

Citizens can apply online by logging in through Sikkim GO, accessing the Marking Order application, completing the required application and tree marking information, attaching the prescribed supporting documents, and submitting the application.

Applicable fee and payment details for the Marking Order service are pending departmental confirmation. Please refer to the official departmental guidelines or contact the department for confirmation.

After submitting an application via Sikkim GO, applicants can monitor the status of their Marking Order request online once application tracking is configured. Please refer to the official departmental guidelines or contact the department for confirmation.

Citizens can visit the Forest and Environment Department at the Forest Secretariat, Deorali, Gangtok, Sikkim, or approach the respective District Helpdesk in their district. Please refer to the official departmental guidelines or contact the department for confirmation.
