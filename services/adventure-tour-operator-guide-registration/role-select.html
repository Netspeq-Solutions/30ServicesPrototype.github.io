Role 1 • Citizen

Citizen

Access the Adventure Tour Operator & Guide Registration service, apply online, track progress across 5 stages, pay statutory fee (₹1,500), and download certificate / logbook record.

  • Two distinct registration streams (ATO & Guide)
  • 5-Step visual progress tracker with SLA dates
  • Simulated ₹1,500 fee payment modal when activated
  • Interactive multi-scenario switcher
Continue as Citizen
Role 2 • Technical Assistant

Department – TA

Scrutinize initial applications, assign Inspecting Officer (IO) for Adventure Tour Operators, verify Guide dossiers, and forward files.

  • HQ TA: Assign IO for ATO gear inspection
  • HQ TA: Review Gangtok Guide 7-point dossier
  • District TA: Scrutinize & forward to District DTO
  • Return for correction & raise formal queries
TA – Headquarters District TA
Role 3 • Inspecting Officer

Department – IO

Conduct mandatory on-site physical gear, safety harness, and equipment inspections strictly for Adventure Tour Operators (ATO).

  • Exclusive to Adventure Tour Operator flow
  • UIAA/EN safety gear certification checks
  • Record inspection date, result & field remarks
  • Return verified inspection report to HQ TA
Continue as IO
Role 4 • Assistant Director HQ

Department – Asst. Dir.

Statewide technical oversight and concurrence desk. Reviews ATO inspection reports, Gangtok Guide dossiers, and other-district dossiers before HQ DTO sanction.

  • Review IO safety reports for ATO applications
  • Review Gangtok Guide dossiers forwarded by TA
  • Review paid District Guide files for HQ issuance
  • Provide technical concurrence & forward to DTO
Continue as Asst. Dir.
Role 5 • Tourism Officer (DTO)

Department – DTO

Sanctioning authority. Activates statutory fee (₹1,500), reviews paid files, grants final sanction, and issues Certificates / ID Cards & Manual Logbooks.

Selected Office: Gangtok (HQ)
HQ DTO sanctions ATO & Gangtok Guides, and issues final Logbook for all districts.
  • Activate statutory fee (₹1,500) for applicant
  • HQ DTO: Final approval & Certificate issuance
  • HQ DTO: Digital ID & Manual Logbook recording
DTO – Headquarters District DTO
