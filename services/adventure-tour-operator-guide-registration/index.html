Official Guidelines & Requirements

Comprehensive specifications for adventure enterprises and tourist guides in Sikkim

Prerequisites

Who Can Apply

Eligibility criteria depend strictly on the selected registration type.
A. Adventure Tour Operator:
  • Registered firm, agency, or resident enterprise operating in Sikkim.
  • Employ certified technical instructors/guides with proven adventure credentials.
  • Possession of verified safety gear, first-aid equipment, and insurance coverage.
  • Compliance with Sikkim Adventure Tourism Safety Regulations.
B. Guide Registration:
  • Individual resident or domicile of Sikkim (minimum age: 18 years).
  • Completion of recognized mountaineering / trekking guide training (HMI / NIM / IITTM / Dept. Course).
  • Physical and medical fitness certification from a registered medical practitioner.
  • Valid police verification / character clearance.
Specific requirements are pending final departmental notification.
Checklist

Required Documents

Supporting documents differ by registration type and are pending confirmation.
For Adventure Tour Operators:
  • Certificate of Incorporation / Partnership Deed / Trade License.
  • Registered Office address proof within Sikkim.
  • Credentials and training certificates of lead adventure instructors.
  • Equipment safety inspection report & public liability insurance.
For Guide Applicants:
  • Proof of Identity & Domicile (Certificate of Identification / Voter ID / Aadhaar).
  • Certified Mountaineering / Guide Course Completion Certificate.
  • Medical Fitness Certificate issued by Government Hospital / Registered Doctor.
  • Police Clearance / Character Verification Certificate.
  • Recent passport-size photographs.
Prepare supporting documents in clear PDF/JPEG formats.

Frequently Asked Questions

Instant answers to common questions about adventure tour operator and guide registration in Sikkim

The Adventure Tour Operator & Guide Registration service is a unified single-window digital platform established by the Tourism & Civil Aviation Department, Government of Sikkim. It enables commercial adventure tourism agencies and individual tourist guides to apply for, renew, and receive official statutory registrations and accreditation online.

The portal provides two distinct registration options:
  • Adventure Tour Operator: For commercial enterprises, firms, or agencies organizing outdoor adventure sports such as trekking, mountaineering, rafting, paragliding, or mountain biking.
  • Guide Registration: For individual professional guides providing certified guiding and interpretive services to tourists across Sikkim.

If you represent a business entity, travel company, or agency that organizes commercial adventure activities, choose Adventure Tour Operator. If you are an individual professional guide seeking certified accreditation to guide visitors, select Guide Registration. Do not combine their applications.

Document requirements differ by category. Adventure Tour Operators typically require business registration, Sikkim office proof, instructor qualifications, and equipment safety compliance. Guides require proof of identity/domicile, recognized institute training certificates (HMI/NIM), medical fitness, and police clearance. Exact specifications are subject to official departmental confirmation.

Statutory fee schedules for Adventure Tour Operators and Guides are determined by the Tourism & Civil Aviation Department under applicable state tourism rules. Official prescribed fees are pending formal gazette notification and will be displayed during application submission.

Click the "Login via Sikkim GO" button to authenticate with your Sikkim GO Single Sign-On credentials. Once logged in, select your registration category, complete the online application form, upload the necessary credentials, and submit digitally.

After submission, you will receive a unique Application ID. You can track the status of departmental verification, inspection schedules, and approval decisions directly from your citizen dashboard at any time.

You can contact the Tourism & Civil Aviation Department at Paryatan Bhawan, Tadong, Gangtok via telephone at 03592-209090 / 1800-345-3256, or by email at secy-tourism@sikkim.gov.in. You may also contact any of the six District Tourism Offices listed below.
