        Help and Support

        Common questions

        • Rent for each financial year is due by 31 March. A year paid late attracts a 5% surcharge.
        • Advance payment for future years is not accepted.
        • An offline challan is valid for 15 days and never past 31 March. After paying at the bank, upload the Bank Receipt from Upload Bank Receipt in the menu.
        • Arrears before your ULB went live are a certified opening balance from the paper register.
