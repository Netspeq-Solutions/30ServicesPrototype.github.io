Payment Options

Two Ways to Pay Your Ground Rent

The amount always goes to the selected site's account. Anyone may pay for any site, as with utility bills.

Option A

Pay Online

Pay the full amount shown on your bill through the payment gateway. Your receipt with a QR code is generated as soon as the payment succeeds, and the site is saved under My Ground Rent Accounts for one-tap payment next year.

  • UPI, debit / credit card or net banking
  • Receipt sent by SMS and email
  • If the result is pending, the payment is shown as under verification until it is confirmed
Gateway pending confirmation Login to Pay Online
Option B

Pay Offline (Bank Receipt)

Generate an offline payment challan, pay the exact amount at the Department's designated bank, then upload a scan or photo of the Bank Receipt (BR). The Department verifies it and issues your receipt.

  • Challan valid for 15 days, and never past 31 March
  • Upload the BR scan; staff verify it and issue your receipt
  • Advance payment for future years is not accepted

Service Details

Official Guidelines & Requirements

The paper Assessment Chart of Ground Rents is now online. Each ULB joins once its register records are loaded.

Coverage

Who Pays Ground Rent

  • Allottees of urban sites recorded in the Department's ground rent register.
  • Joint allottees – any one of them can pay for the site.
  • Anyone paying on behalf of an allottee; the money always goes to the selected site.
  • Heirs of a deceased allottee may pay in the old name until the transfer is recorded.
No new allotments are made through this service.
Checklist

What You Need

No documents need to be uploaded to pay ground rent.
  • Sikkim GO login: your mobile number and OTP.
  • Any one search detail: GR ID from an earlier receipt, plot no., allottee's name or father's / husband's name (at least 3 letters).
  • Plot number is optional: most register entries have none, so search by name works too.
  • For offline payment: the printed challan and the original Bank Receipt.
Sites linked to your mobile number appear automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about paying ground rent in Sikkim

It replaces the paper Assessment Chart of Ground Rents. You can find your site, see what is due year by year and pay online or through a Bank Receipt. Department staff maintain allottees, structures and transfers in the same system.

Type any one of these in the search box: plot no. (if recorded), allottee's name or father's / husband's name (at least 3 letters), or the GR ID printed on an earlier receipt. Case, dots and extra spaces are ignored, so "M.D. Ibrahim" and "md ibrahim" both match. Pick your site from the list; no amounts are shown in the list, to protect privacy.

The Ground Rent ID is the permanent number of your site, for example GR-GMC-W05-00123 (ULB, ward and serial number). It never changes, even when the allotment is transferred, and it is printed on every receipt, challan and SMS.

Rent for a financial year is due by 31 March at the end of that year. Paying any time within the year is on time. A year paid after its due date attracts a 5% surcharge. Whether this is charged once per overdue year or for every year of delay is pending departmental confirmation.

No. Advance payment has been stopped. The bill always runs up to and including the current financial year, never a future year.

Choose Pay Offline on your bill to get a challan. It is valid for 15 days, and never past 31 March because the amount changes on 1 April. Pay the exact amount at the Department's designated bank, then upload a scan or photo of the Bank Receipt on the challan page. Staff verify it and issue your system receipt; keep the original BR in case the office asks to see it. The designated bank is pending departmental confirmation.

Dues for years before your ULB joined the system are taken from the paper register. The Department calculates and certifies them as an opening balance. The system calculates rent and surcharge itself only from the go-live year onward. For questions on the opening balance, contact your ULB helpdesk.

No. Transfer applications are processed at Department headquarters outside this system; staff only record the transfer once the order is issued. Refunds are also handled outside the system. If you pay more than the billed amount offline, only the billed amount is recorded and the excess is flagged for refund.
