Who Needs a Licence

Two Types of Applicant

Choose your applicant type and the purpose of the licence. The form and the documents asked for follow your choice.

Type 1

Individual / Firm

A firm, enterprise or establishment of the applicant(s) – a proprietor or partners – for an activity / item in the UDD notification, for example:

  • Agricultural and forest produce
  • Grocery, provisions and dairy
  • Meat, fish and egg sellers
  • Departmental stores and garments
  • Tailors, dyers and footwear
  • Beauty parlours, spas and gyms
  • Automobile showrooms, workshops and garages
  • LPG distributors and petrol pumps
27 items in the form, many with sub-items; the department keeps the list up to date as notified.
Type 2

Registered Company

Pvt. Ltd., Public Ltd., societies and other registered entities, for:

  • Manufacturing
  • Security agency / manpower placement
  • Banking & non-banking
  • Departmental stores
  • Casino
  • Hotels / restaurants
  • Pharmaceutical companies
  • Others (describe the business)
Circles: choose the circle where your premises are. The North-East Circle is run from UDD Headquarters, Gangtok. The South-West Circle (Soreng, Jorethang and other offices) will be added once its workflow is confirmed. Only officers of the circle you choose act on your application.

Official Guidelines & Requirements

A licence is valid for one financial year (1 April – 31 March) and must be renewed every year.

Checklist

Documents for a New Licence

Individual / firm

  • Photo, and one of SSC / COI / RC
  • Building Occupancy Certificate, NOC of the house owner (format provided)
  • Panchayat / Councillor recommendation and undertaking (formats provided)
  • Fire & Emergency Services NOC, unemployed certificate

Registered company

  • Land Parcha of the premises, Certificate of Incorporation, MoA, board resolution, company PAN
  • PVR, Pollution Control Board clearance, Law Department enlistment, Fire NOC

Plus licences or NOCs for the item, for example FSSAI for food, Animal Husbandry for meat shops, Traffic Police for garages and car parks.

Full document list by item & downloadable formats
Fees

Licence Fee & Payment

  • The Inspector works out the licence fee and the garbage fee at the site inspection, as per the UDD notification for your activity.
  • Amount to pay = licence fee + garbage fee. It is shown to you once the application is approved.
  • Pay at the bank and upload the Bank Receipt (BR). The full fee applies whichever month the licence is issued.
  • Bring the BR and original documents to the department, unless the Inspector already saw the originals at the site visit.
Your first licence fee becomes your renewal amount; the department may revise it.
Renewal

Renewal & Late Fee

  • Licence expires on 31 March. Renewal opens a month or two before (as set by the Department).
  • Submit the renewal request with the GST certificate. You can add or remove items at the same time. No fee is paid with the request.
  • No late fee by 15 May (45 days' grace). After that: ₹5 per day, counted to the date you first submit (e.g. 4 June → ₹100).
  • The Inspector works out the fee and the BO approves. Then generate the challan, pay, upload the BR and download the renewed licence.
No cut-off: however late, you renew through the same flow.

Digital Workflow

How to Get Your Licence (5 Steps)

You can track your application at every stage from your dashboard

1

Apply Online

Log in through Sikkim GO, choose the applicant type, item and sub-item, fill the form and upload the documents asked for.

2

Inspection

An Inspector of your circle checks the documents, visits the site and assesses the licence fee, garbage fee and any other charges.

3

Review & Approval

The BO reviews; the Joint Director / Director, the Special Secretary or the Issuing Authority approves. If anything needs correction, it is returned to you with remarks.

4

Challan & Bank Receipt

Generate the challan, pay it at the bank and upload the BR. Bring your original documents if asked.

5

Download Licence

Once the BR is verified, download your QR-verifiable e-Trade Licence.

Renewals: submit the request; the Inspector works out the fee and the BO approves. Then pay by challan and upload the BR.
