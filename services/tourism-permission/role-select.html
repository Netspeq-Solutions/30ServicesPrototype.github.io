Select Your Access Role

Select any role below to evaluate the complete Tourism Permission workflow across Citizen, Tourism Officer (TO), DTO, HQ Officer, and Department Admin desks.

Role 1 • Citizen

Citizen / Applicant

Submit applications across 9 categories, track progress, pay activated fees, request extensions, and download Approval Sheets & Certificates.

  • Submit & respond to returned remarks
  • Complete post-approval payment
  • Request extensions (Drone & Event/Mela/Fair/Rallies/Marathons)
Continue as Citizen
Role 2 • Tourism Officer (TO)

Tourism Officer (TO)

Conduct initial review, return applications with remarks, redirect miscategorised filings, update Drone flight parameters, and forward to DTO/HQ.

  • Update Drone Period, Altitude (AGL) & Area
  • Redirect miscategorised applications
  • Forward to DTO or HQ Officer
Continue as TO
Role 3 • District DTO

District Tourism Officer (DTO)

Process DTO-level applications, activate payment, issue DTO final approval, forward Drone/Expedition to HQ, and decide extension requests.

  • Activate payment & grant DTO final approval
  • Forward HQ-level apps to HQ Officer
  • Decide Extensions (incl. Drone Permission)
Continue as DTO
Role 4 • HQ Officer

HQ Officer

Single HQ Officer handling both initial review (approve for payment / reject / return) and final approval (Approval Sheet upload + Certificate) for Drone & Expedition.

  • Initial review & payment activation
  • Receives app back after citizen payment
  • Upload Approval Sheet & complete final approval
Continue as HQ Officer
Role 5 • Dept Admin

Department Admin

Maintain the official Liaison Officer (LO) directory—add, edit, and update LO names, contact details, addresses, and phone numbers.

  • Add & edit Liaison Officers
  • Update phone, email & address details
  • Live sync to Citizen Guidelines & Forms
Continue as Dept Admin
