Prerequisites

Who Can Apply

Eligibility requirements are subject to the applicable departmental guidelines.
  • Individual trekkers, expedition organizers, or registered travel & tour agencies.
  • Organizers of cultural events, exhibitions, fairs, melas, rallies, or marathons in Sikkim.
  • Pilots, production teams, or cinematographers operating unmanned aerial vehicles (Drones).
  • Operators and promoters organizing adventure tourism sports within designated zones.
Review the applicable departmental guidelines.
Checklist

Required Documents

Document rules are category-dependent and pending official confirmation.
  • Fire NOC: Applicable for public events, fairs, exhibitions, and temporary structures.
  • Environment NOC: Applicable for eco-sensitive trekking routes and wildlife sanctuaries.
  • Land NOC: Required for events conducted on municipal, revenue, or forest lands.
  • Any Other Supporting Document: Route itinerary, identity credentials, or activity outline.
Prepare required supporting documents in PDF/JPEG.

Citizen Helpdesk

Frequently Asked Questions

Instant answers to common questions about tourism permissions in Sikkim

The Tourism Permission service is a single online service through which applicants can request tourism-related permissions for activities and events requiring departmental approval from the Tourism & Civil Aviation Department, Government of Sikkim.

Any individual, organization, expedition team, drone pilot, or event coordinator intending to organize trekking, mountaineering expeditions, public fairs/melas/marathons, drone shoots, or commercial adventure activities within Sikkim must apply for departmental permission prior to commencement.

Trekking and Expedition categories require a statutory government fee of ₹100 each. Permissions for Events/Fairs/Melas/Rallies/Marathons, Drone operations, Adventure Activities, and Any Other activities are currently Free. Online payments can be made through SBI ePay or UPI.

Depending on the activity category, required documents may include Fire NOC (for public events and temporary structures), Environment NOC (for eco-sensitive and forest zones), Land NOC (for revenue or municipal land usage), and other activity-specific clearances. Document rules are category-dependent and pending official department confirmation.

The statutory service timeline is pending official confirmation by the department. Processing involves digital verification of submitted documents and coordination with field officers or inter-departmental bodies where necessary. Applicants receive automated status notifications at each step.

Upon approval by the competent departmental authority, an official digitally signed permission letter will be generated and made available for instant download in PDF format on your citizen dashboard.

Each digitally issued permission letter features a unique QR code and reference number. Law enforcement personnel, forest guards, or tourism checkpoint officers can scan the QR code using any smartphone camera to view live verification credentials directly on the official Government of Sikkim portal.
