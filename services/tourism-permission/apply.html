Departmental Instructions & Terms for Drone Operations in Sikkim

1. Airspace Classification & Clearances: All drone operations must comply with DGCA UAS Rules, 2021 (Green, Yellow, and Red Zones). Operations inside Red Zones, international border perimeters, and defence installations are strictly prohibited without prior Ministry of Defence / Home clearance.

2. Qualified Remote Pilots & UIN: Only DGCA-certified Remote Pilots holding a valid Remote Pilot Certificate (RPC) and operating a Drone with a valid Unique Identification Number (UIN) are permitted to apply.

3. Safety, Privacy & Sensitive Locations: Maintain visual line-of-sight (VLOS) within permissible Altitude (AGL) approved by the Tourism Officer. Respect privacy of local residents, monasteries, and protected ecological habitats. Zero disturbance to wildlife is mandatory.

4. Validity & Extension: Approved Drone Permission is valid for 15 days. An extension of up to 15 additional days may be requested and will be decided by the District Tourism Officer (DTO).
