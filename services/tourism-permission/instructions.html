Mandatory Step Before Applying

Instructions for Tourism Permission Application

Please read the following instructions carefully before proceeding with your application.

A

Select the Correct Application Category

The applicant must select the category that matches the proposed tourism activity. Ensure you apply under the appropriate category to avoid delays or returned applications.

Trekking
Permit for high-altitude trekking routes & trails
Expedition
Mountaineering peaks & restricted alpine zones (Requires Liaison Officer)
Events, Fairs, Melas, Rallies & Marathons
Public gatherings, cultural rallies, marathon events
Drone
Aerial filming, videography & drone operations
Adventure Activities
River rafting, paragliding, rock climbing, mountain biking
Any Other
Other tourism permissions requiring departmental clearance
Satellite Phone Usage Permission
Statutory telecommunication clearance in high altitude / remote zones
B

Provide Correct Applicant Information

Applicants should provide accurate personal and contact information. Your full name, mobile number, email address, residential address, and identity proof must correspond with official government records. All official notifications and permission status updates will be communicated through these credentials.

C

Provide Activity Details

Applicants should provide clear details about the proposed activity, including its purpose and relevant information. This includes dates of activity, proposed itinerary or venue, route details, number of participants, guide or tour operator details, and safety arrangements.

D

Supporting Documents

The application may require supporting documents depending on the activity nature and venue:

Fire NOC
Environment NOC
Land NOC
Any Other NOC
Important Note on Documents: The exact document requirements depend on the application category and applicable departmental guidelines. Not all documents are mandatory for every category. Clear guidance will be provided during the category-specific form submission.
E

Application Fee Structure

The statutory fee applicable for each Tourism Permission category is as follows:

Application Category Applicable Fee Special Requirement
Trekking ₹100 Standard trail permit
Expedition ₹100 Requires Liaison Officer
Events, Fairs, Melas, Rallies and Marathons Free NOC verification
Drone Free Airspace security clearance
Adventure Activities Free Safety audit check
Any Other Free General assessment
Satellite Phone Usage Permission Free Provisional free fee (Prototype)

*Note: Only Expedition requires Departmental Liaison Officer selection during the application process.

F

Application Review

Applications will be processed through the concerned departmental authorities (District DTO verification and Headquarters review). Applicants should track their application status regularly using the online tracker.

G

Communication

Applicants should provide valid contact information to receive application-related communication, clarification queries, or verification remarks.
