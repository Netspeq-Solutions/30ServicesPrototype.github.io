Guidelines, Terms & Application Instructions

Please review the permission categories, document requirements, fee rules, and Liaison Officer directory carefully before submitting an application.

1

Permission Categories & Required Documents

Each category below lists its applicable fee, validity, and highlighted mandatory documents (Max 5MB PDF/JPG per file).

Drone Permission

Free
Validity: 15 Days • Extension: Up to 15 days
Required Documents
  • Drone UIN & Remote Pilot Certificate *
  • Flight Area Map & Coordinates Sheet *

Expedition Permission

₹100 / person
Validity: Requested Duration • No Extension
Required Documents
  • Expedition Route Plan & Peak Clearance Dossier *
Optional: Medical Fitness, Insurance, Guide Details, Clients Sheets • LO confirmation required (see Section 2)

Trekking

₹100 / person
Validity: 10 Days (Max 10 pax, Min 2 porters) • No Extension
Required Documents
  • Passport * & Visa (if foreign national)
  • Medical Insurance *
  • Photograph of Each Trekker & Tour Operator *
Optional: Medical Fitness, Additional Insurance, Guide Details, Clients Sheets

Event / Mela / Fair / Rallies / Marathons

Free
Validity: 1 Week (7 Days) • Extension: Up to 1 week
Required Documents
  • Fire NOC *
  • Police NOC *
  • Councillor / Panchayat NOC *

Satellite Phone Permission

Free
Validity: As Approved • Extension: Not Applicable
Required Document
  • Letter of Recommendation issued by IMF * (only required document)

Other Permissions

Free
Validity: As Approved • Extension: Not Applicable
Supporting Document
  • Activity Supporting Document / Clearance (optional, as applicable)
