Prerequisites

Who Can Apply

Eligibility requirements depend on the selected registration type and applicable departmental guidelines. Please refer to the official requirements before applying.
  • Travel Agents: Resident citizens or registered commercial entities with an established office and local trade license.
  • Tour Operators: Operating travel firms possessing registered business premises, fleet tie-ups, and qualified tour managers.
  • Dealers: Commercial shop/office premises with Shop Act registration and verified equipment stock. Subject to mandatory physical inspection.
  • Outdoor Photographers: Individual applicants (18+ years) with minimum Class X educational certificate, verified training credentials, and portfolio.
Categorized criteria per registration type.
Checklist

Required Documents

Supporting documents differ according to the selected registration type. Confirmed and pending requirements are distinguished below.
  • Identity & Address Credentials: Aadhaar / Voter ID / COI / Sikkim Subject Certificate (All Types).
  • Business Proof (TA/TO): Municipal Trade License, Firm Registration, Bank Solvency Certificate.
  • Commercial Premises Proof (Dealers): Shop Act Registration, Rent Agreement, Conditional House-Owner NOC.
  • Qualifications (Photographers): Class X Certificate (Mandatory), Photography Diploma/Training proof, Recommendation Letter.
Prepare supporting documents in clear PDF or JPEG format.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about tourism business and photographer registration in Sikkim

It is a unified, single-window online service established by the Tourism & Civil Aviation Department, Government of Sikkim, through which applicants can apply for statutory registration as Travel Agents, Tour Operators, Dealers, or Outdoor Photographers.

There are four distinct registration categories: (1) Travel Agent Registration, (2) Tour Operator Registration, (3) Dealer Registration, and (4) Outdoor Photographer Registration. Applicants must select the specific category applicable to their business or professional activity.

Review the category descriptions and prerequisites on this portal. If you arrange passenger tickets and travel itineraries, choose Travel Agent. If you conduct and organize guided group tours, choose Tour Operator. If you sell or rent trekking, mountaineering or adventure equipment, choose Dealer. If you provide commercial photography or cinematography services, choose Outdoor Photographer.

Supporting documents differ by registration type. All applicants require identity and address proof. Commercial entities require trade licenses, Shop Act registration, and premise lease/ownership documents. Outdoor photographers require minimum Class X educational proof, photography training/credentials, and recommendations. Please consult the checklist section for detailed requirements.

Statutory fees and service delivery timelines are determined by the department in accordance with official state notifications and gazette orders. The applicable fee schedules and statutory processing timelines are currently pending official gazette confirmation.

Dealer applications require valid Shop Act registration, verified business premises, stock inventory, and a mandatory on-site physical inspection by an authorized Inspecting Officer prior to departmental approval.

Applicants must be at least 18 years old, possess a mandatory minimum Class X educational certificate, and furnish verified certificates of training, experience portfolio, and professional recommendations in photography or cinematography.

Click on 'Apply for Registration' or 'Login via Sikkim GO' on this portal. Once signed in via Sikkim GO, select your required registration type, complete the guided multi-step form, upload your supporting documents, and submit.

After submission, an Application Reference Number (ARN) will be issued. You can track real-time verification and scrutiny status directly through your citizen dashboard by logging in via Sikkim GO.

You can contact the Tourism & Civil Aviation Department Headquarters at Paryatan Bhawan, Tadong, Gangtok (Phone: 03592-209090 / 1800-345-3256, Email: secy-tourism@sikkim.gov.in) or visit your respective District Administrative Centre.
