Statutory Registration Guidelines & Fee Matrix

Requirements under the Sikkim Registration of Tourist Trade Rules • Valid for 1 Calendar Year

Category 1 • TA/TO

Travel Agent / Tour Operator

₹1,500 – ₹20,000 / 1 Year
  • Valid Municipal Trade License & Commercial premises
  • Grade A (₹20k), B (₹7k), C (₹5k), D (₹1.5k)
  • 3-Tier Workflow: TO → IA → TO → DTO
  • SLA: 15 Working Days
Category 2 • DLR

Equipment Dealer

₹2,000 / 1 Year
  • Physical commercial premises ≥ 200 sq.ft
  • Certified equipment inventory & storage
  • Level 1 IA On-Site Inspection
  • SLA: 21 Working Days
Category 3 • PHO

Outdoor Photographer

₹1,500 / 1 Year
  • Class X pass certificate & ID Proof
  • Portfolio of Sikkim outdoor work & camera serial
  • Direct 2-tier approval (TO → DTO)
  • SLA: 7 Working Days

Required Clearances & Documents

Travel Agent / Tour Operator

With Inspection
  • COI / Sikkim Subject / Domicile (Mandatory)
  • Municipal / Panchayat Trade License & PAN
  • Office Ownership Deed or Lease Agreement
  • MoA / Partnership / Affidavit / Qualification (Optional)

Equipment Dealer

With Inspection
  • Certificate of Identification / COI (Sole Mandatory Document)
  • Equipment Inventory & Safety Standards Compliance
  • On-site premises verification conducted by Level 1 IA

Outdoor Photographer

Without Inspection
  • Proof of Identity & Class X Educational Certificate
  • Photography Training & Recommendation (Optional Experience / Paragliding)
  • GST Certificate not required
