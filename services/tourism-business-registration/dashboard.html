Required Clearances & Documents

Prepare digital scans of the following statutory clearances prior to application submission.

Notice: Document rules are category-dependent and pending official department confirmation.

1. Fire Safety NOC

Public Events & Fairs

Mandatory for fairs, cultural melas, rallies, temporary stages, sound pavilions, and sporting marathons involving public gatherings. Must be obtained from the local District Fire Station.

2. Environment / Forest NOC

Eco-Sensitive Corridors

Required when proposed routes or activity zones fall within national parks, sanctuaries, reserved forest boundaries, or designated high-altitude biosphere zones under Forest & Environment Department guidelines.

3. Land Revenue / Municipal NOC

Venue & Land Clearance

Mandatory for events, temporary camping, or staging held on municipal property, Urban Development Department grounds, or District Administrative Centre public spaces.

4. Any Other Supporting Document

Activity Specific

Route map, participant identity credentials, safety and rescue protocol, medical insurance, or pilot UIN registration certificate (for drone operators).
