How the Online Application Process Works

Standard 3-step digital delivery under Sikkim Public Service Guarantee

1. One-Time Citizen Registration

Create citizen profile with mobile OTP and address details for auto-fill in all services.

2. Submit Form & Attach Documents

Complete service-specific forms and upload required supporting proofs in PDF or JPG format.

3. Track & Download Certificate

Track real-time officer scrutiny and download digitally signed QR-coded certificates.
